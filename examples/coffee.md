# Coffee

Coffee is one of the most widely consumed beverages in the world, brewed from roasted seeds of the Coffea plant.

## Origins

According to legend, coffee was discovered by an Ethiopian goat herder named Kaldi around the 9th century, who noticed his goats became energetic after eating red coffee cherries. From Ethiopia, coffee spread to Yemen, where the port city of Mocha became an early center of the coffee trade.

## Species

Arabica accounts for roughly 60 to 70 percent of world production. It has a smoother, more complex flavor and grows best at high altitude. Robusta contains about twice as much caffeine as arabica, tastes more bitter, and is more resistant to disease and pests.

## Brewing

The ideal water temperature for brewing coffee is between 90 and 96 degrees Celsius. Water that is too hot extracts bitter compounds; water that is too cool leads to weak, sour coffee.

Espresso is made by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee at about 9 bars of pressure for 25 to 30 seconds. A french press steeps coarse grounds in hot water for about 4 minutes before pressing. Cold brew steeps coffee in cold water for 12 to 24 hours, producing a smooth drink with lower acidity.

## Drinks

A cappuccino is made of equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and milk foam. An americano is espresso diluted with hot water. A flat white is espresso with thin, velvety steamed milk and little foam.

## Decaf

The Swiss Water Process removes caffeine without chemical solvents, using only water, temperature, and osmosis. It preserves most of the coffee's original flavor.
